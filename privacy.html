Privacy Policy

01 Introduction

Welcome to PSWeb Studio. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry or communicate with us about our web development services.

By using our website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

02 Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when using our website or contacting us.

Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Contact number
  • Company or business name

Project Information

  • Project requirements and descriptions
  • Preferred project timeline
  • Estimated budget or service preferences
  • Reference websites and additional information you share

Technical Information

Certain technical information may be collected automatically, including browser type, device information, operating system, IP address and general website usage information, depending on the tools enabled on our website.

03 How We Use Your Information

The information collected may be used for the following purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries and project requests.
  • To understand your business and project requirements.
  • To prepare proposals, quotations and estimates.
  • To communicate about services and ongoing projects.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To improve website performance and user experience.
  • To maintain website security and prevent misuse.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

We use your information only for legitimate business purposes and in accordance with applicable laws.

04 How We Share Your Information

PSWeb Studio does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share limited information with service providers when necessary to operate our website and business.

  • Netlify: Website hosting and form submission processing.
  • Supabase: Database infrastructure and information storage.
  • Analytics providers: Website traffic and performance analysis, if enabled.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and applicable service terms.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable laws, regulations or valid legal requests.

05 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality and understand how visitors interact with our website.

These technologies may help us:

  • Remember website preferences.
  • Understand visitor behaviour.
  • Measure website traffic and performance.
  • Improve website functionality.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

06 Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

We use appropriate hosting and database infrastructure to support our website and enquiry management processes.

However, no method of online transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted over the internet.

07 Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy.

This may include:

  • Responding to project enquiries.
  • Managing project discussions and client relationships.
  • Maintaining relevant business records.
  • Meeting applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal obligations.

08 Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, including client websites, social media platforms and third-party services.

PSWeb Studio does not control the privacy practices, content or security of external websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of external websites before sharing personal information.

09 Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.
  • Raise concerns about how your information is handled.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details provided below. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

10 Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses, organisations and individuals seeking web development services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our business purposes.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

11 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business practices, website functionality or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page along with the revised Last Updated date.

Note: We encourage visitors to review this page periodically to stay informed about how their information is handled.

12 Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact PSWeb Studio.
