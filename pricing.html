Frequently asked questions

Find answers to common questions about our website development services.

Are the package prices fixed?

Displayed prices are starting prices. The final quotation is confirmed after reviewing your requirements and agreeing on the project scope. Any additional work is quoted before development begins.

Are domain and hosting included?

Domain registration and hosting are separate expenses. They are registered in the client's name to ensure ownership and control.

Can I add more pages or products?

Yes. Additional pages, products and features can be included at an agreed additional cost.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Every package includes responsive layouts designed for mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Do you provide SEO services?

All packages include foundational on-page SEO setup. Advanced SEO campaigns, ongoing optimization and content marketing are quoted separately. No ranking position is guaranteed.

Can I update website content myself?

Static websites require developer assistance for content changes. Dynamic content management and admin dashboard functionality can be included in custom projects.

What are the payment terms?

Our standard payment structure is 50% advance, 30% after design approval and 20% before final launch. Custom projects may follow milestone-based payments.

What support is available after launch?

Each package includes a defined post-launch technical support period for eligible fixes. New features, additional development and ongoing maintenance are quoted separately.

Who owns the website after completion?

The client owns the completed website and agreed deliverables after full payment, subject to applicable third-party software and asset licenses.
