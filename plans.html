Plan questions

Do I need a plan after launch?

No. Every website package includes a free support window for fixes. A plan is for ongoing updates, backups, monitoring and SEO work once that window ends.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. Plans run month to month. You can move up, move down or stop with 15 days' notice.

What counts as a content update?

Changing text, swapping images, adding a package or attraction card, or editing an existing page. New page templates or new features are quoted separately.

Do unused hours carry over?

No. Hours reset every month so response times stay predictable for all clients.

Can you maintain a site you did not build?

Yes, after a short audit of the code, hosting and security. If the site needs fixes first, those are quoted before the plan starts.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us about your site and we will recommend one. No lock-in.
