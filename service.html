Quick answers

Questions clients ask first

Short answers on timelines, mobile support and maintenance. The full list is on the FAQ page.

How long does a website take to launch?
Standard business sites usually take 7 to 14 days. Larger portals, booking engines and CRM dashboards run 2 to 3 weeks with milestone reviews. Content that arrives late is the most common cause of delay.
Will it work on all phones and browsers?
Every site is built mobile-first and tested on current Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge, plus phone and tablet screen sizes, before launch.
Why custom code instead of WordPress or a page builder?
Page builders and heavy themes often load scripts and styles a page never uses, which slows mobile loading. Hand-written pages carry only what they need and give exact design control. The trade-off: content changes go through a developer unless you add an admin area.
Is there support after launch?
Yes. Each package includes a post-launch support period (14 to 60 days by tier) for bug fixes and small edits. Ongoing maintenance can be arranged separately.
