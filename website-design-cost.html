Four ways to keep the cost down

These choices make the biggest difference to the quote.

Send content readyFinal copy and images on day one remove writing and sourcing time from the quote.
Reuse page templatesTwenty pages on one layout cost far less than five pages with five layouts.
Build in phasesLaunch the core pages first, then add booking or dashboard features once the site is earning.
Skip features you won't useEvery integration adds testing time. Add them when a real need shows up.

Common questions

How much does a website cost?

A five-page business site starts at ₹14,999. A 15-page site with enquiry forms and technical SEO starts at ₹29,999. Sites with an admin dashboard start at ₹59,999. The final number depends on pages, content and features.

Why do two websites with the same page count cost different amounts?

Page count is only one factor. Custom layouts, database-backed forms, listings with filters and migration from an old site each add build time.

Does the price include a domain and hosting?

No. Domain and hosting are billed at cost and registered in your name, so you keep full ownership.

How long does a website take?

A five-page site usually takes one to two weeks. A 15-page site takes three to four weeks. Dashboard projects take six to eight weeks. Late content is the most common cause of delay.
