About the Studio

Web Products Engineered for Performance, Visibility and Growth

PS Web Studio is a digital engineering studio founded in 2023 and based in Mumbai, India. We design and build websites, web platforms and business systems for clients across travel, healthcare, enterprise services and growing commercial brands, delivered remotely to clients in any region.

Our work covers corporate websites, dynamic portals, booking and enquiry systems, CRM and admin dashboards, and technical SEO. Every project follows the same standards: clean code, mobile-first interfaces, fast load times and a structure search engines can read.

To build reliable, high-performing web products that remove complexity for our clients and turn their websites into working business assets.

How We Work

A Four-Stage Delivery Process

A defined workflow with a written scope, so you know what is being built, when, and for how much.

Stage 01

Discovery & Scope

We review your goals, audience and competitors, then define the site structure, features and a written scope.

Stage 02

UI/UX Design

Clean layouts with readable typography, clear calls to action and mobile-first navigation, approved before development.

Stage 03

Development

Standards-compliant code with optimised assets, validated and secured forms, and back-end logic where the project needs it.

Stage 04

QA, SEO & Launch

Cross-browser testing, Core Web Vitals review, schema setup, deployment to your domain and a documented handover.
